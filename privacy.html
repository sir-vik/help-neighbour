Privacy Policy

Learn how Neighbourly handles information when you use our platform.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Neighbourly is a platform designed to help people connect with neighbours who may need assistance or have useful skills to offer.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use Neighbourly and how that information may be used.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Neighbourly, the platform may collect or receive information such as:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account role and profile information.
  • Skills or services you choose to offer.
  • Information contained in help requests.
  • Messages exchanged through the platform.
  • Images or profile information you choose to upload.
  • Location information when location features are used.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Help connect requesters with suitable helpers.
  • Display relevant requests and profile information.
  • Enable communication between users.
  • Improve the functionality and security of Neighbourly.
  • Investigate reports of inappropriate behaviour.
  • Maintain and administer the platform.

4. Location Information

Some Neighbourly features may use location information to help users discover relevant requests or helpers nearby.

Location access is intended to support the platform's local-connection features. Users should avoid sharing more precise personal location information than is necessary.

5. Information You Share With Other Users

Information that you choose to place on your profile, include in a request, or send through a conversation may be visible to other users as necessary for the relevant Neighbourly feature.

Do not share passwords, financial credentials, security codes or other highly sensitive information with other users.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Neighbourly may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies where necessary to support authentication, preferences, security and application functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

Neighbourly may rely on third-party technology providers to provide services such as authentication, database storage, hosting, maps or other technical functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by the platform. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Neighbourly is not intended to be used by children in violation of applicable age requirements. Users should meet the minimum age requirements communicated during registration or required by applicable law.

10. Your Choices

You may choose what information you provide through your profile and requests, subject to information required for the platform to operate.

You should also review your profile and requests regularly and avoid publishing unnecessary personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Neighbourly develops. When significant changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information on Neighbourly, please use the contact method provided through the platform.
