Community Guidelines

Help make Neighbourly a respectful, useful and safer community for everyone.

Our Community

Neighbourly exists to help people ask for assistance, share useful skills and build positive connections with people around them.

Every user has a role in keeping the community respectful and responsible.

What we encourage
  • Respectful communication.
  • Honest and accurate requests.
  • Clear communication about expectations.
  • Respect for people's privacy.
  • Helpful and constructive behaviour.
  • Reporting concerning behaviour when necessary.

Our Core Guidelines

01
Be respectful

Treat other users with courtesy. Do not harass, threaten, insult or intimidate other members.

02
Be honest

Provide accurate information about yourself, your skills and the help you are requesting.

03
Respect privacy

Do not share another person's private information, photographs or personal details without appropriate permission.

04
Communicate clearly

Explain requests clearly and discuss important details before agreeing to a job or meeting.

05
Protect your information

Never share passwords, PINs, verification codes, banking credentials or other sensitive information with another user.

06
Use the platform responsibly

Do not use Neighbourly to commit fraud, distribute harmful content, impersonate people or conduct unlawful activities.

Behaviour we do not allow
  • Threats or harassment.
  • Fraud or scams.
  • Impersonation.
  • Hate or abusive conduct.
  • Sexual harassment.
  • Sharing private information without permission.
  • Malicious or harmful content.
  • Attempts to compromise accounts or the platform.
  • Illegal use of the service.

Meeting Other Users

If you decide to meet another Neighbourly user in person, use reasonable caution.

  • Consider meeting in an appropriate public location when possible.
  • Tell someone you trust about your plans when appropriate.
  • Do not share unnecessary personal information.
  • Trust your judgment if a situation feels unsafe.
  • For specialized or regulated work, use qualified professionals where required.

Reporting a Problem

If you experience or observe behaviour that violates these guidelines, use the reporting tools provided by Neighbourly where available.

Reports may be reviewed and appropriate action may be taken to protect the community.

Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, violations may result in content removal, warnings, account restrictions, suspension or termination.

Serious matters may also be referred to appropriate authorities where required by law.

Keep Neighbourly Helpful

A strong community depends on people treating each other with respect. Before posting, messaging or agreeing to help someone, consider whether your actions contribute to a useful and respectful environment.
