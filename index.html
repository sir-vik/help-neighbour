About Neighbourly

Stronger communities start with people.

Neighbourly is built around a simple idea: people often have skills that can help someone else nearby.

Instead of making it difficult to find the right person, Neighbourly creates a place where people can ask for help, offer their skills and build useful local connections.

Frequently asked questions

Questions? We've got you.

Here are some of the things new Neighbourly users may want to know.

Who can use Neighbourly?
Anyone who meets the platform's registration requirements can create an account and use Neighbourly to request help or offer useful skills.
Can I both request help and help other people?
Yes. Neighbourly is designed so users can participate as people who need help, people who offer help, or both.
Does Neighbourly set the price of repairs?
No. The appropriate cost for a job can depend on the actual situation, materials, location and work required. Users should discuss and agree on relevant details before work begins.
How do I find a helper?
Create a request describing what you need. Neighbourly can then help surface relevant nearby people based on the information available on the platform.
How do I stay safe?
Review the information available about another user, communicate clearly about the request, protect your personal information and use available reporting tools when necessary.
Is Neighbourly a replacement for professional services?
Neighbourly is a platform for connecting people. For work that requires a licensed or specially qualified professional, users should use an appropriately qualified professional.
